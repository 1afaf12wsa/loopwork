Terms

Last updated 22 November 2026. Polish version: Regulamin. Refunds in short: Refunds.

These terms explain how working with Loopwork works: asking for a free chapter, the two ways to have a book made, payments and refunds, and buying one of the books we sell. Our privacy notice explains what we do with your data.

1. Who we are

Loopwork is run by Volodymyr Verbytskyi, Kielce, Poland, email hello@loopworkbooks.com. Loopwork is not a registered company: Volodymyr runs it as an individual under the Polish rules for unregistered activity (działalność nierejestrowana).

In these terms, "we" and "us" means Loopwork. "You" means the creator we make a book with, or a reader who buys one of our books.

2. Using this website

  • The site is free to use. You need an up-to-date web browser, and an email address to ask for a free chapter.
  • When you send the form, you ask us to look at your public posts and reply to you about a possible book. That is all the form does. It ends once we have replied, or earlier if you ask us to stop.
  • Please don't send anything unlawful, or anyone else's details, through the form.

3. The free chapter

We read your public posts, suggest a book idea with an outline, and write and design one real chapter. It costs nothing, and you don't owe us anything for it. If you don't go ahead, we don't publish or sell that chapter anywhere.

4. How we agree to work together

Before any work on the full book starts, we confirm in writing (an email is enough): the book, the deal you chose, the price or split, and that these terms apply. That exchange and these terms are our agreement. If we also sign a separate written agreement with you, it applies wherever it differs from these terms.

5. Share the sales (no upfront cost)

  • You pay nothing. We write, design and launch the book, and we split net sales 50/50 from the first sale. During Black Week the split is 60/40 in your favour (section 7).
  • "Net sales" means what buyers pay, minus sales tax or VAT where it applies, card and platform fees, refunds and chargebacks.
  • The book sells through our shared Whop page. Whop splits each sale automatically and puts your share into your own Whop balance, which you can withdraw whenever you like.
  • You get three review rounds plus a final check.
  • It is your book, with your name and your content. While we work together, it is sold only through the shared page.
  • Ending it: before launch, either of us can stop at any time by telling the other in writing, with nothing to pay. After launch, either of us can end it with 30 days' written notice. Sales during the notice period are split as usual, and then the book comes off sale. If you would like to keep selling it yourself, we can agree a buyout of our share. Readers keep the copies they bought.

6. Own it outright (one-time fee)

  • You pay a one-time fee per book and keep every sale, after store fees. The fee is $199 a book for your first 3 books if you start by 31 December 2026. From 1 January 2027 it is $290 a book. During Black Week it is $149 (section 7). We confirm your price in writing before you pay anything.
  • You pay half when you start and half when you approve the finished book. Payments go through Whop.
  • You get two review rounds plus a typo check.
  • Once you have paid in full, the book is yours to sell or give away anywhere. We set up the sales page in your own Whop account and give you the final files.
  • If you don't approve the book, you don't pay the second half, and we refund the first half (section 9).

7. Black Week offer, until 30 November 2026

  • Share the sales: you keep 60% of net sales and we keep 40%, for as long as that book sells with us.
  • Own it outright: $149 a book instead of $199.
  • To get it, ask for your free chapter, or tell us you want to go ahead, by 30 November 2026 at 23:59 Polish time. Then start the book by 31 December 2026: for Share the sales, by confirming in writing; for Own it outright, by paying the first half.

8. Timing and changes

  • The free chapter usually takes a few days. A full first draft takes about two weeks, then as long as your reviews take. These are estimates, not fixed deadlines. If something delays us, we tell you.
  • Changes beyond the review rounds of your deal, new chapters, or a new edition later are agreed with you before we do them.

9. Refunds and your right to withdraw

  • Own it outright: until you approve the book, you can stop at any time. If you stop, or don't approve the finished book, we refund the half you paid upfront in full, within 14 days of you telling us, to the payment method you used. If we can't deliver the book for any reason, we refund everything you paid.
  • Share the sales: you don't pay us anything, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Your legal right to withdraw: if you are a consumer, or a sole trader for whom this agreement is not part of your professional activity, you can withdraw within 14 days of making the agreement, without giving a reason. Email us, or use the model form at the end of these terms. Our refund promise above goes further than this right: until you approve the book, you get your upfront payment back in full. If you ask us to start straight away and then approve the finished book and pay in full within those 14 days, the service is complete and the right to withdraw ends.

10. Your content, the book and AI

  • Your content stays yours: your writing, photos, methods and name. You let us use it only to make, sell and promote your book, and we only publish promotion you have approved.
  • You confirm that the content you give us is yours, or that you have the right to use it.
  • We keep your unpublished material private and use it only for your book.
  • We draft text with AI tools, and we edit every page by hand against your own posts. Some illustrations may be made with AI. We tell you which ones before you approve the book.
  • We show your book to other people as an example of our work only with your OK.
  • We don't make books that give medical, legal or financial advice.

11. Buying one of our books

  • Our books are digital (PDF). Whop runs the checkout and delivers the file.
  • Books on our Whop page (whop.com/loopworkbooks) are sold by us together with the author. Books on an author's own Whop page are sold by that author, under their own terms.
  • For a book bought from our page: if you change your mind, ask within 14 days of buying and we refund you. If the file doesn't open, or the book isn't what its page described, we fix it or refund you. Email hello@loopworkbooks.com from the email you bought with.

12. Complaints

Email hello@loopworkbooks.com, tell us what went wrong and what you would like us to do. We reply within 14 days. If you are a consumer, you can also get free help from a consumer ombudsman (in Poland, the miejski or powiatowy rzecznik konsumentów) or the Trade Inspection (Inspekcja Handlowa). More information is at uokik.gov.pl. In the EU, the European Consumer Centre in your country can help with cross-border problems.

13. Responsibility

We don't promise any number of sales. Each of us pays our own taxes on our share. If something goes wrong because of us, our responsibility is limited to what you paid us for that book. This limit doesn't apply to your rights as a consumer, or to harm caused on purpose or through gross negligence.

14. Law, language and changes

  • Polish law applies. If you are a consumer living in another country, you keep the protections your own country's law gives you.
  • If we disagree, we first try to sort it out by talking. If that doesn't work, the court that has jurisdiction under the law decides.
  • These terms exist in English and in Polish (Regulamin). They say the same thing. If they ever differ, the Polish version prevails.
  • We may update these terms. The version in force when you start a book applies to that book, unless you agree to a change. The date at the top shows the last update.
