How it works

Four steps, and most of the work is ours.

  1. 1

    We read your last few months

    Your posts, captions and comments. We find the one problem your audience keeps coming back to, and turn it into a book idea with a full outline.

  2. 2

    You get a free chapter

    Before you commit to anything, we write and design one real chapter from your own content, so you can judge the quality instead of taking our word for it.

  3. 3

    We write and design the book

    First draft in about two weeks. You tell us what's wrong with it and we keep fixing until it sounds like you. Nothing goes out without your yes.

  4. 4

    We launch it and the money splits itself

    We handle the page, the checkout, the tax and the refunds. You share it with your audience. Every sale is split the moment it happens and your half lands in your own account, so you are never waiting on us to pay you.

Is it for you

We would rather say no than waste your time.

This works when

  • You teach something, even if you never call it teaching
  • People ask how you did it, in comments and DMs
  • You sell physical things, or your time, or nothing at all yet
  • You post consistently to an audience that engages

This does not work when

  • Your page is reposts, memes or pure entertainment
  • You already sell a course or book covering the same ground
  • Your followers come only to buy, never to learn
  • The subject is medical, legal or financial advice

The deal, in plain words

You pay nothing, at any stage. We carry the writing, the design, the store and the admin. When the book sells we split the profit down the middle, and that split is open to discussion before we start.

Your content stays yours. The book is built from it and published with your approval, and if you ever want out, it comes down.

Questions

Who actually writes it?

We do. We draft with AI tools, which is how we move fast and charge you nothing upfront, then we edit every page by hand against your own posts. The expertise is yours throughout, and nothing is published until you have read it and said yes.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. Not the writing, not the design, not the store. We only make money when the book does, which is the same incentive you have.

Who owns the book and my content?

Your content stays yours, always. The book is published with your approval, and if you want it taken down, it comes down.

How and when do I get paid?

On every sale, automatically. The store splits each purchase and sends your share straight to your own balance, so you withdraw it whenever you like without asking us. Half the profit is yours by default, and we are happy to discuss the split before we start.

How long does it take?

A free sample chapter within a few days. A full first draft in about two weeks, then however long your revisions take.

What if I don't like it?

Then it does not get published. That is why the first chapter is free and why nothing moves without your approval. You are never committed to something you have not read.
