How it works

Four steps, and most of the work is ours.

  1. 1

    We read your last few months

    Your posts, captions and comments. We find the one problem your audience keeps coming back to, and turn it into a book idea with a full outline.

  2. 2

    You get a free chapter

    Before you commit to anything, we write and design one real chapter from your own content, so you can judge the quality instead of taking our word for it.

  3. 3

    We write and design the book

    First draft in about two weeks. You review it in rounds, and nothing goes out without your yes.

  4. 4

    We launch it and the money comes straight to you

    We handle the page, the checkout, the tax and the refunds. You share it with your audience. Your share of every sale lands in your own account the moment it happens, so you are never waiting on us to pay you.

Is it for you

We would rather say no than waste your time.

This works when

  • You teach something, even if you never call it teaching
  • People ask how you did it, in comments and DMs
  • You sell physical things, or your time, or nothing at all yet
  • You post consistently to an audience that engages

This does not work when

  • Your page is reposts, memes or pure entertainment
  • You already sell a course or book covering the same ground
  • Your followers come only to buy, never to learn
  • The subject is medical, legal or financial advice

Questions

Who actually writes it?

We do. We draft with AI tools, which is how we move fast and keep the cost low, then we edit every page by hand against your own posts. The expertise is yours throughout, and nothing is published until you have read it and said yes. Photos come from your own posts where they matter, and some illustrations are made with AI; the agreement says which.

What does it cost me?

Our pick is the 50/50 deal: you pay nothing upfront, and we only earn when your book sells. If you'd rather pay once and keep every sale, it's a one-time $199 a book. The first chapter is free either way, so you can read it before you choose.

Who owns the book and my content?

Your content stays yours, always, and the finished book is your book. On the 50/50 deal it sells through our shared page while we work together, and if you want out, it comes down. With the $199 it's all yours once paid.

How and when do I get paid?

On every sale, automatically. Whop, the store, puts your share straight into your own Whop balance, and you withdraw it to your bank whenever you like without asking us. Your share is all of each sale with the $199, or half on the 50/50 deal. We are happy to discuss the split before we start.

How long does it take?

A free sample chapter within a few days. A full first draft in about two weeks, then however long your revisions take.

What if I don't like it?

Then it does not get published. That is why the first chapter is free and why nothing moves without your approval. You are never committed to something you have not read. If you chose the $199 option and don't approve the book, we refund the half you paid upfront. Our refund policy.
