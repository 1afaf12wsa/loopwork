Privacy

Loopwork is run by Volodymyr Verbytskyi in Poland. If you have a question about your data, email hello@loopworkbooks.com.

If you use the form on this site

We collect your email address and your Instagram handle. We use them to look at your public posts and to reply to you about a book. We don't sell them, and we don't add you to a newsletter.

If we emailed you first

We found your address where you published it for business contact, and wrote to you about a possible book. Reply "no thanks" and we won't write again.

Who helps us

Cloudflare hosts this site, Resend delivers form messages to our inbox, and Google runs our email. If you become a client or a buyer, Whop handles the store and payments.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we're talking to you or working together, and for as long as we need them for our records after that.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold about you, and you can object to us using it. Email us and we'll do it. You can also complain to the Polish data protection office (UODO).
