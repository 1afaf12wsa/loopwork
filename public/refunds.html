Refunds

Last updated 22 November 2026. The full rules are in our terms (po polsku).

If we make a book with you

  • Own it outright: you pay half when you start. Until you approve the finished book you can stop at any time, and if you stop or don't approve it, we refund that half in full. If we can't deliver the book for any reason, we refund everything you paid.
  • Share the sales: you pay nothing upfront, so there is nothing to refund.
  • If you are a consumer, you also have a legal right to withdraw within 14 days of making the agreement. Our promise above already covers it. How it works.

If you bought one of our books

  • Bought from our Whop page (whop.com/loopworkbooks): changed your mind? Ask within 14 days of buying and we refund you.
  • If the file doesn't open, or the book isn't what its page described, we fix it or refund you.
  • Books on an author's own Whop page are sold by that author, under their own refund terms.

How to ask

Email hello@loopworkbooks.com with what you bought or agreed, and the email you used. We reply within 14 days and refund to the payment method you used.
